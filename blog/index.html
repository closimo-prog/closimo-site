B2B pipeline leakage is the silent loss of deal value at every stage between a lead and a closed contract. Most companies measure their close rate. This hub covers all 5 stages with 2026 benchmark data across 120 industries — from SaaS to Healthcare Tech to Logistics — with localised cost calculations so you can see what each leak costs your business annually.
