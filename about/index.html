Our story

In January 2025, I had a serious accident. It forced me off my feet and gave me something I hadn't had in years. Time to be still.

I'd spent years running SMA Business Connect, helping companies with operations, finance and HR. But sitting with every client file I'd ever worked, I kept seeing the same thing. 80% of every business problem traced back to one place, their sales process. Not their team. Not their product. The process. And they had no idea what it was costing them.

That's when CLOSIMO was born. Not from ambition. From stillness. From being forced to stop and actually look at the data.

Our method

Nine steps. The first two are the audit. The rest is the fix.

  • 01Audit

    We study your pipeline, your people, your training, your incentives and your structure.

  • 02Diagnose

    We find the root causes and put a cost on every leak.

  • 03Design

    We build the sales system that closes those leaks.

  • 04Train the team

    We train your people to run the new system.

  • 05Implement

    We put the system to work inside your business.

  • 06Measure

    We track the numbers that show if it works.

  • 07Learn the gaps

    We find what still leaks and why.

  • 08Improve

    We fix what the numbers show.

  • 09Repeat

    We run the loop again until the results hold.
